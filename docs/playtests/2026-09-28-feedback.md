## By tester

### Kyle — played 5:54

- Wouldn't pick it up alone ("not my type of game"), but would play if asked.
- **Likes the 30-second checkpoints.**

  accomplishment, a steady stream of small rewards.

## Ideas to consider

Grouped by area. These are testers' suggestions, not decisions.

**Controls**
- Lower steering sensitivity and/or add smoothing and a dead zone, on both
  joystick and gyro.
- Reconsider the pedal input: hold, rhythm/timing, or fast tapping (Jake
  expected hold; Kyle suggested rhythm).

**Onboarding and level 1**
- Push new players to the tutorial first.
- Make level 1 gentler: straight downhill start, wider road, no wind yet.

**Boost and feedback**
- Make boost easier to discover.
- Let boost stack over time (2× → 3×) with feedback that feels good each step.
- Graded pedal feedback (perfect / great), rhythm-game style.

**Failure and pacing**
- Keep the 30-second checkpoints.
- Let the death cone cost boost or a life rather than end the run.
- Re-examine whether the time limit earns its place.

**Longer-term**
- Co-op for couples and friends (three separate asks).
- Mastery and speedrun support: leaderboards, co-op speedruns, hard
  "Kaizo"-style levels, level sharing like Mario Maker.
- Story mode.
